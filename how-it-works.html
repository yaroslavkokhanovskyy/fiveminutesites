How it works

From one quick call to a live website.

You're busy running your business, so your part takes about 5 minutes. we handle everything else.

  1. 1
    Day 1 · 5 minutes

    We have a quick call

    We ask you 10 short questions about your business. If you're on Google or Facebook, we fill in what we can find first, so you just confirm it. Send a logo and a few photos if you have them.

  2. 2
    Days 1–3

    We design and build your site

    We write the wording, pick colours and fonts that fit your business, and build a site that works on every phone and computer. You don't have to do anything.

  3. 3
    Within 2–3 business days

    You get a free preview

    We send you a link to your finished website. Look it over on your phone and tell us anything you'd like changed.

  4. 4
    When you're happy

    You approve and pay

    Only once you love it do you pay the flat $399. No deposit, no surprises.

  5. 5
    Launch day

    Your site goes live

    We set up your web address and hosting in your name and put the site online. Then we help you add the link to your Google listing and social pages.

No homework

Here's everything we'll ask you.

Short answers are perfect. Not sure about something? Just say "you choose."

  1. Your business in one sentenceWhat you do and where you serve
  2. How customers reach youPhone, email, address, hours, social pages
  3. The #1 thing visitors should doCall, book, get a quote, visit or order
  4. Your main services or productsYour top 3 to 6, prices optional
  5. Why customers choose youExperience, speed, price, warranty, anything
  6. Your typical customerWho you serve most
  7. The feel you wantModern, friendly, bold, elegant or classic
  8. Your coloursLogo colours, or ones you love or hate
  9. A few real reviewsFrom Google, Facebook or happy customers
  10. Your logo and photosPhone photos are fine
