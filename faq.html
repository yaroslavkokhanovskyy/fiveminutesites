Quick answers to what business owners ask most. Don't see yours? Just call or text us.

Getting started

What do I need to send you?

Just your answers to 10 short questions. A logo and a few photos help, but they're optional. We can use clean placeholder images you can swap later.

Do I really only spend 5 minutes?

Yes. One short call where we ask the questions and type your answers. You'll also spend a few minutes looking at your preview.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Definitely. If your current site is outdated or doesn't work well on phones, we'll rebuild it with a fresh, modern design for the same price.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any local business in Ontario: restaurants, trades, salons, clinics, studios, shops and professional services.

Pricing and payment

Do I really pay nothing until I see it?

Yes. We build your preview first. You only pay the $399 once you've seen it and you're happy with it.

Are there monthly fees?

Not from us. The only running costs are your web address (about $15–25 a year) and hosting, which is often free for a site like this.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Tell us what you'd like adjusted on the preview, and we'll update it before we launch.

Ownership and setup

Who owns the website and web address?

You do. Your web address is registered in your name, and the website is yours to keep.

What are hosting and a domain?

The domain is your web address, like yourbusiness.ca. Hosting is where the site lives online. We set both up for you; you just pay those small costs directly.

Will customers find me on Google?

Your site is set up with the basics Google looks for, like your business name, location and services. We'll also help you add it to your Google Business Profile.

Call or text us. We're happy to answer anything, no pressure.
